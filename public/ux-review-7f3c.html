Draft Analyzer UX Review

Thank you for testing Draft Analyzer. Please approach the app as someone with no fantasy-football knowledge. The goal is to find unclear language, hidden actions, unexpected behavior, and places where the interface assumes too much.

Testing note: Draft Analyzer now supports both guest browser storage and Supabase accounts. Start signed out to test the guest experience, then create a test account to evaluate cloud storage and guest-data transfer. For a completely isolated test, use a private browsing window. Knowing this page's URL grants access to it; the page is unlinked and excluded from search indexing, but it is not password-protected.

What changed since the original review

Accounts and cloud storage

Users can now sign up, sign in, sign out, and access their draft pools and imported drafts across devices through Supabase.

Guest mode remains available

Signed-out users still work entirely in browser storage. After signing in, they can merge guest drafts into their account or permanently delete the browser copies.

Deeper analysis

Rankings now include confidence, draft ranges, refined meaningful-pass weighting, faster pool switching, individual player pages, and improved navigation between related drafts and players.

Account and storage tests

Guest workflow

Remain signed out, import drafts, create and rename Draft Pools, assign drafts, and refresh the page.

Expected: Guest data persists in this browser, while the header identifies you as a guest and offers a sign-in link.

Account workflow

Create a test account or sign in, then create, rename, assign, and delete account data.

Expected: The header shows the account email, the account page confirms the session, and saved data returns after signing out and back in.

Guest-data transfer

Import data while signed out, then sign in and use the browser-data banner. Test the confirmation screens before choosing whether to move or delete the guest data.

Expected: Moving merges new data without changing existing account drafts. Duplicate source files are skipped, and browser copies disappear only after a successful transfer.

Suggested test

  1. Begin signed out. Explore the home page and describe what you believe the app does.
  2. Import both CSV samples at the same time, choose UX Tester Team, and save them.
  3. Create two Draft Pools, rename one, and assign drafts to each pool.
  4. Refresh and confirm the guest drafts and pool assignments persist.
  5. Import the working FantasyPros URL, then try importing it again and try the invalid URL.
  6. Sort the saved drafts and confirm the sort control is hidden when only one or no drafts exist.
  7. Open an imported draft and test its position, team, and player-search filters.
  8. Confirm the individual draft page includes the site header and allows its pool assignment to change.
  9. Open Personalized Rankings and test pool, position, search, and sortable-column controls.
  10. Switch between a small and large Draft Pool and note whether filtering feels responsive.
  11. Open a player page and review Ranking Confidence, overall and personal draft ranges, meaningful passes, and team appearances.
  12. Follow both the related-player and draft links from a meaningful pass and confirm each opens the expected page.
  13. Apply a manual ADP adjustment, exclude and restore a player, and download the rankings CSV.
  14. Enable Draft Tracker Mode and cycle players through Available, Mine, and Unavailable.
  15. Create a test account or sign in and inspect the guest-data transfer confirmation.
  16. Move the guest data into the account and confirm drafts, pools, and assignments remain intact.
  17. Sign out and confirm account drafts disappear; sign back in and confirm they return.
  18. Try the experience at a narrow/mobile browser width.
